## 3. Notable Therapeutic Deep Dives

### **A. Advanced Gene Therapies**
* **Otarmeni (lunsotogene parvec-cwha):** First-ever dual AAV vector-based gene therapy approved for sensorineural hearing loss associated with OTOF gene variants. It targets genetic causes of profound childhood hearing loss.
* **Kresladi (marnetegragene autotemcel):** Autologous gene therapy introducing a functional copy of the ITGB2 gene in hematopoietic stem cells to treat severe Leukocyte Adhesion Deficiency type I (LAD-I).

### **B. Chronic Weight Management: Foundayo (orforglipron)**
* **Mechanism:** Small-molecule, non-peptide oral GLP-1 receptor agonist.
* **Clinical Significance:** Unlike peptide-based injectables or older oral peptides (like semaglutide), orforglipron can be taken at any time of day with or without food. It represents a significant improvement in patient convenience and manufacturing scalability.

### **C. Chronic Hepatitis Delta: Hepcludex (bulevirtide-gmod)**
* **Mechanism:** Viral entry inhibitor blocking NTCP receptors on hepatocytes.
* **Clinical Significance:** Marks the first-ever FDA-approved therapy for chronic HDV, which affects patients coinfected with HBV and previously had no targeted therapeutic options.

### **D. COVID-19 Post-Exposure Prophylaxis: Xocova (ensitrelvir)**
* **Mechanism:** 3CL protease inhibitor.
* **Clinical Significance:** First oral antiviral approved specifically for preventing symptomatic COVID-19 after contact with an infected individual.
