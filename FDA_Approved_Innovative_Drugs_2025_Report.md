# FDA Approved Innovative Drugs Report - Full Year 2025
**Report generated on June 5, 2026**

This report compiles the key novel drug and biologic approvals (New Molecular Entities, New Therapeutic Biologics, and Cell/Gene Therapies) approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) between January 1, 2025, and December 31, 2025. It reviews regulatory milestones from both the Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) and the Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER).

## 1. Executive Summary

The year 2025 was a landmark period for pharmaceutical innovation, with the FDA approving **46 novel drugs** via CDER, along with major cell and gene therapy breakthroughs approved via CBER. 

Key milestones of the 2025 approvals cohort include:
*   **The Non-Opioid Pain Management Breakthrough**: Approval of **suzetrigine (Journavx)**, marking the first time in over 20 years a first-in-class non-opioid analgesic has been approved for moderate to severe acute pain.
*   **Major Gene and Cell Therapy Advancements**: Approval of **Waskyra** (for Wiskott-Aldrich Syndrome), **Encelto** (first allogeneic encapsulated cell implant for macular telangiectasia), and **Zevaskyn** (for recessive dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa wounds).
*   **Hereditary Angioedema (HAE) Expansion**: Simultaneous approvals of oral on-demand treatments like **sebetralstat (Ekterly)** and highly effective monthly subcutaneous prophylactics like **garadacimab (Andembry)** and **donidalorsen (Dawnzera)**.
*   **First-in-Class Antimicrobials**: Approvals of **gepotidacin (Blujepa)** for uncomplicated UTIs and **zoliflodacin (Nuzolvence)** for uncomplicated urogenital gonorrhea, representing critical options against drug-resistant pathogens.

## 3. Notable Therapeutic Deep Dives

### **A. Pain Management Reform: Journavx (suzetrigine)**
*   **Mechanism**: Selective NaV1.8 voltage-gated sodium channel blocker. NaV1.8 is expressed primarily in peripheral nociceptive neurons, which transmit pain signals.
*   **Clinical Significance**: By blocking sodium channels selectively in the periphery, suzetrigine halts pain propagation without entering the central nervous system. This yields effective acute pain relief without any opioid-like addiction risks, respiratory depression, or typical central side effects.

### **B. Hereditary Angioedema (HAE) Therapies**
The year 2025 introduced both advanced on-demand oral options and highly convenient monthly injectables:
*   **Ekterly (sebetralstat)**: The first oral, small-molecule plasma kallikrein inhibitor approved for on-demand treatment of acute HAE attacks. It allows patients to self-administer a pill immediately upon sensing an attack, bypassing the need for intravenous or subcutaneous rescue injections.
*   **Andembry (garadacimab-gxii) & Dawnzera (donidalorsen)**: Monthly subcutaneous antisense and monoclonal therapies offering sustained prophylactic prevention of HAE attacks, drastically reducing the baseline attack frequency.

### **C. Cellular and Gene Therapies (CBER)**
*   **Waskyra (etuvetidigene autotemcel)**: An autologous gene therapy for Wiskott-Aldrich Syndrome (WAS) that inserts a functional WAS gene into hematopoietic stem cells, offering a functional cure for patients lacking matched bone marrow donors.
*   **Encelto (revakinagene taroretcel-lwey)**: A first-in-class encapsulated cell implant that continuously secretes ciliary neurotrophic factor (CNTF) directly into the vitreous to arrest macular degeneration in MacTel patients.
